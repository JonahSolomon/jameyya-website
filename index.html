How Jameyya works

From scattered chaos to organized trust in three steps.

1

Create a group or pool

Pick a name, set the frequency or contribution amount, and customize how it looks.

2

Invite your people

Share a link via WhatsApp. They join in seconds — no account required to start.

3

Let Jameyya handle the rest

Smart reminders, fair rotation, transparent history. You stay focused on the people, not the spreadsheet.

Built for the way we actually share

Whether you call it Jameyya, Committee, Chit, Hagbad, or rotating savings — these traditions are part of everyday life across the GCC, MENA, South Asia, and many global diaspora communities. Jameyya brings that trust online, without losing the human warmth that makes it work.

⚠️ Important: Jameyya is a tracking tool, not a financial service. Jameyya does not hold, transfer, or process any money. All actual transfers between members happen outside the app, directly between trusted parties. Always exercise due diligence and only join pools with people you trust.
