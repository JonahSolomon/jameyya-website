Delete Your Account

Jameyya — Money Pools (com.jameyya.app), by Younis Al Sulaimi

Delete your account from within the app

  1. Open the Jameyya app and sign in.
  2. Go to the Settings tab.
  3. Scroll to the very bottom of the Settings screen and tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm the deletion. Your account and its data are removed immediately.

Or request deletion by email

If you can no longer access the app, email us at dev@jameyya.com from the email address linked to your account, with the subject "Account Deletion Request". We will process the request within 30 days and confirm by reply.

What is deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes: your profile (name, email address, phone number if added, avatar), your sign-in identities, your memberships in pools and groups, your comments, uploaded proof images, notification history, and your device push tokens.

What may be kept

Records of pools you participated in that belong to other members (for example, a pool's contribution ledger maintained by its admin) may retain non-identifying entries so the remaining members' records stay accurate. Payment records for App Store / Google Play purchases are kept by Apple/Google according to their own policies. We keep no other data after deletion.

لحذف حسابك: افتح تطبيق جمعية ← الإعدادات ← انزل إلى أسفل الشاشة ← حذف الحساب ثم أكّد الحذف. سيتم حذف حسابك وبياناتك (الملف الشخصي، العضويات، التعليقات، الصور، سجل الإشعارات) نهائياً. إذا لم تستطع الدخول إلى التطبيق، راسلنا على dev@jameyya.com من بريدك المسجّل وسنعالج الطلب خلال ٣٠ يوماً. قد تبقى سجلات غير معرِّفة للهوية في دفاتر الجمعيات الخاصة بالأعضاء الآخرين حفاظاً على دقة سجلاتهم.
